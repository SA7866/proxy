About Proxy

Wear what you mean.

Proxy is a custom print-on-demand brand built around one idea — your clothing should say something. Not someone else's logo, not a generic graphic. Yours.

We give you the tools to design directly on the product, pick your colours, add your text, upload your images, and see exactly what you're getting before you order. No guesswork, no minimum quantities, no waiting on a designer.

✎

Our live canvas editor lets you place text and images directly onto the product. What you see is what gets printed — no surprises.

Made to Order

Every item is printed when you order it. No warehouses full of stock that nobody asked for. Just your design, on your product, shipped to you.
